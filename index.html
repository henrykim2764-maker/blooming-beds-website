How it works

  1. Send the form below. A rough size is enough.
  2. We come by, measure and give you an exact price. That part is free.
  3. We plant, water everything in and send you a photo of the finished job.
  4. You pay after you've seen it, by Venmo, cash or check.

Want fresh plants every season? Ask about the Four-Season Plan. We come back each season, each visit is priced like that season's package, and you can stop any time.

Questions

Do I need to be home?

No. If we can reach the bed and a hose, we can plant while you're out.

Will it survive the cold?

Pansies and kale handle frost, and many pansies bloom again in spring. We only plant what's made for cold weather.

Where do you work?

Plymouth Meeting, Lafayette Hill, Conshohocken, Whitemarsh, Flourtown, Blue Bell and nearby.
