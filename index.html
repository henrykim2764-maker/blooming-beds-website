Questions

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can reach the bed and a hose, we can plant while you're out. We'll send you the after photo.

Will fall flowers survive the cold?

Pansies and ornamental kale handle frost well, and many pansies bloom again in spring. We only plant what's built for the cold.

How do I pay?

After the job is done and you've seen the bed, by Venmo, cash or check. We don't take credit cards. We agree on the price before we start.

Where do you work?

Plymouth Meeting, Lafayette Hill, Conshohocken, Whitemarsh, Flourtown, Blue Bell and nearby Montgomery County.
